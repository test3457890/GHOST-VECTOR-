# Player Briefing: GHOST VECTOR

Experimental relay satellite **GHOST-01** was declared lost eleven years ago. It just woke up and started broadcasting on a dead downlink. The AstroSec Deep Space Recovery Team needs you to work out what it's saying and bring it home.

## How to play

1. Open the recovery terminal your organizers give you.
2. **Phase 1, Ghost Signal:** download `ghost_telemetry.csv`. Three flags are hidden in it. Submit any one of them on the terminal to open Phase 2.
3. **Phase 2, Silent Vector:** download `vector_telemetry.csv` and `starmap.png`. Recover four navigation marker codes. They look like `NAV_WORD_WORD_N`.
4. **HANDSHAKE.SYS:** enter all four codes. When they lock, go to a judge.
5. **Judge:** explain how you solved each marker. If you pass, you get an alignment slate.
6. **Transmit:** use the slate to build the master coordinate and send it.

## Flag format

- Phase 1 flags: `AstroSec{...}`
- Phase 2 codes: submit them on the scoreboard as `AstroSec{NAV_...}`

## Rules

- Don't attack the terminal, the server or other players. Everything you need is in the downloaded files.
- Any tools are allowed. The final stage needs a judge and a physical card.
